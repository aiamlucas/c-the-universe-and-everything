tex_y wrap with & (height - 1):
tex_y = 0..63→ normalin range
tex_y = 64 & 63= 0← tiles back to top
tex_y = 65 & 63= 1← row 1
tex_y = 127 & 63= 63← last row
tex_y = 128 & 63= 0← tiles again
tex_x = fixed for whole strip (where along wall)
tex_y = advances each screen pixel (walks down texture)
x = screen column (0..1919, loop counter)
y = screen row (draw_start..draw_end)
tex_x and x are independent — tex_x depends on where the ray hit the wall, x is just the loop counter. A ray at screen column x=800 might sample texture column tex_x=47 — no proportional relationship.
